# Day 3: How Breadboards Work

## What I learned
- The 5 holes in each numbered column (a–e) are connected together
- The other 5 holes in that column (f–j) are a separate connected group
- The middle gap separates the top half from the bottom half
- Holes along a row (side by side) are NOT connected
- The long rails at the top and bottom (red +, blue −) are connected
  all the way along. They're used for power and GND
- Two parts touch only if their legs are in the same column group

## What I built
LED blinking circuit on a breadboard in Wokwi:
pin 13 → resistor → LED long leg (A) → LED short leg (C) → GND

## Fun fact
It's called a breadboard because early radio hobbyists built circuits
on real wooden bread-cutting boards using nails and thumbtacks.
